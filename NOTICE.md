# Notice and provenance

## What this is

**Product Practice** is an unofficial, self-directed learning aid. It was built from an internal presentation, *Product Management Fundamentals — 12AUG2026*, prepared by the Digital Experience and Solutions Division, Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR).

## What this is not

- It is **not an official Australian Government publication**.
- It is **not** departmental policy, guidance, or a controlled document.
- It does **not** substitute for the Digital Service Standard, the Digital Experience Policy, departmental delivery frameworks, or advice from your policy, privacy, security or assurance areas.
- It carries **no Commonwealth branding** and does not use the Commonwealth Coat of Arms.

Where this material and any official departmental or Australian Government source disagree, **the official source governs**.

The **course content** is a different matter. It is an original synthesis written for this project, but it summarises and restructures material from the source presentation, which remains the property of its authors and, to the extent it is Commonwealth material, is subject to Crown copyright. The MIT Licence does **not** extend to it.

Third-party frameworks are cited, not reproduced. Scrum Guide definitions, the SAFe WSJF formulation, the Digital Service Standard criteria and the learning-science findings are attributed to their sources on the in-app **Sources** page, with links to the primary material.

## Divergences from the source

This course deliberately departs from the source presentation in several places — adding guardrail clauses to templates, separating Beta from Live, covering the Digital Service Standard, and attributing a quotation the deck leaves unattributed. Every departure is listed in the in-app **Divergence register**, so nobody quotes this material and is contradicted by the slides.

## Currency

Government guidance changes. The Sources page carries a review date. Verify anything load-bearing against the current primary source before relying on it in a governance or assurance setting.
